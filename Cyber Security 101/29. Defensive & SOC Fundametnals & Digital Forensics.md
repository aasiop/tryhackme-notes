
# Defensive & SOC Fundametnals & Digital Forensics

### SOC: Detection & Response

**SOC (Security Operations Center)** - centralized team or command center responsible for monitoring and improving an organization's cybersecurity

---
### SOC Roles

- **Level 1 Analyst:** first responder; performs basic alert triage.
- **Level 2 Analyst:** performs deeper investigations and correlates multiple data sources.
- **Level 3 Analyst:** proactively searches for threats and supports incident response, including containment, eradication and recovery.
- **Security Engineer:** deploys and configures security solutions.
- **Detection Engineer:** creates and maintains detection rules.
- **SOC Manager:** manages SOC processes and reports to the **CISO**.
    

---
### Important SOC Processes

**Alert Triage:** determines the severity and priority of an alert using the **5 Ws**:
    - **What** happened?
    - **When** did it happen?
    - **Where** did it happen?
    - **Who** was involved?
    - **Why** did it happen?

---
### SOC Technology

- **SIEM:** collects and correlates logs from multiple sources to **detect** suspicious activity. It mainly provides detection capabilities.
- **EDR:** monitors endpoints in real time, provides detailed visibility, and supports automated response.
- **Firewall:** filters network traffic and blocks unauthorized/suspicious connections.
- Other technologies include **Antivirus, EPP, IDS/IPS, XDR and SOAR**.
    

---

### Digital Forensics

**Digital forensics** is the investigation of digital devices and data to find evidence related to cybercrime.

---
### NIST Forensics Process

1. **Collection** – securely collect evidence without altering it.
2. **Examination** – filter and extract relevant data.
3. **Analysis** – correlate evidence and reconstruct relevant activities chronologically.
4. **Reporting** – document methodology, findings and recommendations.

---
### Evidence Acquisition

- **Proper authorization** is required before collecting evidence; unauthorized evidence may be inadmissible in court.
- **Chain of custody** documents who collected/accessed evidence, when, where it is stored, and what happened to it. It preserves the evidence's integrity.
- **Write blockers** prevent modification of original storage media during acquisition.

---
### Windows Forensic Images

- **Disk image:** bit-by-bit copy of non-volatile storage; data remains after shutdown.
    
- **Memory image:** copy of volatile **RAM**; contains running processes, open files and network connections. It should be collected **before shutdown/restart**.
    
---
### Important Tools

- **FTK Imager:** creates and analyzes disk images.
- **Autopsy:** open-source platform for disk-image analysis, including deleted files, metadata and keyword searches.
- **DumpIt:** creates memory dumps.
- **Volatility:** analyzes memory images using specialized plugins.
